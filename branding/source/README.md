# Canonical source artwork

`akilix-master.png` is the full detailed monitor-lizard master derived from the supplied Akilix reference boards. `akilix-mark-master.png` is the compact raised monitor-head/scope master. These raster masters are authoritative for full and compact artwork; do not replace them with simplistic vector approximations.

The supplied concept boards are preserved under `../reference/` as art direction, not as distributable logos. The wordmark remains editable SVG typography and is composed separately from the artwork.
